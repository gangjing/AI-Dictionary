权威释义与语境例句

Oxford English Dictionary 12th Ed.
1 [心理学 / 职场认知] 复原力，心理韧性，适应力

The capacity to withstand or recover quickly from difficulties, tough conditions, or emotional shock; mental toughness.

"Courage isn't having the strength to go on – it is going on when you don't have strength; that is genuine resilience."
“勇气并非是有力量坚持下去，而是在毫无力量时依然前行；这正是真正的心理韧性。”
"The pandemic tested the mental and operational resilience of modern healthcare teams."
“疫情深度检验了现代医疗团队的心理承压韧性与实战应变能力。”
2 [物理学 / 材料工程] 弹力，回弹性，弹性形变模量

The ability of a substance or material to spring back into shape after elastic deformation, compression, or impact.

"Nylon is widely preferred in mountaineering gear because of its supreme tensile strength and high mechanical resilience."
“由于尼龙具备卓越的抗拉强度和出色的机械回弹性，在登山装备中被极为广泛地采用。”
3 [生态 / 分布式系统] 系统自愈能力，容灾抗扰性

The capability of a strained ecosystem or complex computing topology to absorb perturbation and reorganize while undergoing stress.

"The cloud infrastructure demonstrates high failover resilience through distributed multi-region consensus nodes."
“该云架构通过分布式多地域共识节点展现出极高的故障自愈容灾能力。”
